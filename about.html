Who We Are

Your Partner in
Tech Career Growth

AshishTek is a Florida-based career services and recruitment firm laser-focused on the US technology sector. Founded on the American values of hard work, opportunity, and excellence — we exist to close the gap between raw talent and real opportunity.

Over the years we've built a trusted network of 200+ employer partners and have successfully placed hundreds of professionals into meaningful, high-paying tech careers across the United States.

We don't just place candidates — we transform careers. From the first training session to final onboarding, we are with you every step of the way.
