FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here are the most common ones we receive.

Do I need prior tech experience to apply?›
Not at all! We work with candidates at every level — including complete beginners. Our training programs are designed specifically to take you from zero experience to job-ready in domains like Data Analysis, Business Analysis, DevOps, and more.
How long does the placement process take?›
Timeline varies based on your skill level and target role. On average, candidates who go through our full training and placement program receive their first offer within 3–6 months. Experienced candidates are often placed within 4–6 weeks.
What is the "Book Your Slot" free consultation?›
It's a free, no-obligation 30-minute call with one of our career specialists. We'll discuss your background, goals, and create a preliminary roadmap for your tech career journey — completely free with no commitment required.
Do you work with employers / companies too?›
Absolutely. Our Recruitment Solutions service is designed for businesses seeking top-tier technical talent. We handle sourcing, screening, and coordination — delivering qualified candidates within 72 hours of engagement.
Are you available on weekends?›
Yes! We know your schedule may not fit typical business hours. Our support team is available on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) to answer questions and schedule consultations. We're committed to being there when you need us.
What employment types do you support?›
We facilitate W2, Contract-to-Hire (C2H), Contract (Corp-to-Corp), and Full-Time Employee (FTE) roles depending on candidate preference and employer requirements across all 50 US states.
